Why most first stores never make a sale

Usually it is not effort. It is three avoidable mistakes made alone, with nobody to point them out early.

The wrong product

Picked because it looked cool, not because the numbers said people buy it. We teach you to check demand, margin and shipping before you spend a cent.

A store nobody trusts

Visitors land, look around and leave. You build from a proven structure, and a mentor reviews the store before any ad goes live.

Ads with no plan

Money goes in, nothing comes out, and nobody knows why. You learn what to test, what each number means, and when to stop or scale.

Questions people ask first

Still unsure? Apply anyway. We go through everything on your call before you commit.

Do I need experience?

No. Stage one starts from zero. If you already have a store, we start where you are and fix what is holding it back.

How much money do I need to start?

You will need a budget for your Shopify plan, a few apps and ad testing. We go through realistic numbers for your situation on your call, so you know before you start.

How soon will I get my first sale?

It depends on your product, your budget and how fast you work through the stages. Some students sell in their first weeks of ads, others take longer. We don't promise income, but we make sure you are never guessing.

Can I join from anywhere?

Yes. Students join from many countries, and we show you how to set up payments and suppliers that work where you live.

Is it group or 1-on-1?

Both. You get group lessons, live calls and the community, plus 1-on-1 calls and reviews with a mentor.

What happens after I apply?

We read your answers and reach out on WhatsApp or email to book a short call. If it is a good fit, we get you started.
